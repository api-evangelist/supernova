# Supernova.io

> Supernova.io is a collaborative platform that empowers product teams to create and write content with AI assistance while staying connected to their company's design system.

Supernova is an AI-powered platform for product teams: design system management, documentation, code automation, and collaborative prototyping connected to your tokens, components, and brand.

## Platform

- [Collaborative AI prototyping](https://www.supernova.io/collaborative-ai-prototyping): Prototype and hand over high-fidelity features from your components, layouts, and tokens without terminal, Git, or IDE knowledge.
- [Write with AI](https://www.supernova.io/write-with-ai): Draft PRDs, specs, and release notes in minutes — connected to your prototypes and updated in real time.
- [Documentation](https://www.supernova.io/documentation): Build and mature design system documentation — guidelines, decisions, assets, and code in one place.
- [Design system management](https://www.supernova.io/design-system-management): Connect design and engineering tools into a seamless ecosystem for managing your design system.
- [Ask AI and browse your design system](https://www.supernova.io/ask-ai-browse-your-design-system): Supernova Portal — search design system data and get AI-powered answers for designers and engineers.
- [Code automation](https://www.supernova.io/code-automation): Automate design system workflows, eliminate manual updates, and ship your brand faster.
- [Integrations](https://www.supernova.io/integrations): Pre-built integrations that unify design systems, documentation, and code automation across your stack.
